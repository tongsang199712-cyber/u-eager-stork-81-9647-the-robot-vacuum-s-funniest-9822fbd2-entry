## The bounty

**The Robot Vacuum's Funniest Excuse** — write one line, at most 120 characters, in which a robot vacuum explains why it missed a spot. An AI judge reads the lines in pairs and the funniest excuse takes the reward.

## The entry

> Sorry, I missed that spot — I was under the couch rethinking my life choices and the dust won.

94 characters, one single line, no trailing newline.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, validates it against the gate (1–120 characters, single line, no trailing newline), reads it back, and compares it byte-for-byte.

## Why this line

The vacuum speaks in its own voice and owns the failure: it hid under the couch to rethink its life choices — and still lost to the dust. The humour comes straight from a vacuum's world (the couch, the dust, the missed spot), and it lands in a single line with no setup the reader has to supply.
